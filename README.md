# timbot
A discord bot that can reply to simple questions, and play music!
This bot simply scrapes Google for responses to questions, and gives the answer to certain questions.

Note that certain questions may not be given an answer.
TimBot responds properly to questions regarding:
* Time/Time Zones
* Geographical Locations
* Math
* Historical Persons

This can be done by simply typing:
.tim <any phrase or query>

Answers to unrecognized questions will simply be met with the answer that TimBot does not know.


TimBot is now capable to streaming music, however this feature is still in development and issues may be experienced.
* .timplay {YouTube URL or search phrase} to play or queue a song.
* .timskip to skip the current song.
* .timpause to pause the current song.
* .timresume to resume a song after pausing.
* .timkill to remove TimBot from a voice channel.
* .timqueue to view the queue of music.

  *Note that unexpected bugs arise, due to connection problems.
